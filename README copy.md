**Race Planner (MVP)**

A stripped-back web app for planning the fuel and pacing of a trail or ultra race, segment by segment.

You create a race and set one number: your carb target in grams per hour. Then you break the race into segments, typically one per stretch between aid stations. For each segment you enter a name, a predicted time, and optionally a distance, which gives you the pace you'd need to hold. A free-text pacing plan sits alongside it for notes like "hike the climbs, run the flats".

Under each segment you build a nutrition list on the fly. Type an item name and its carbs, press Enter, and it's added. There's no product library to maintain and no timer. Items can be reordered so the list doubles as the sequence you'll actually eat in.

The app does one calculation: predicted time multiplied by your hourly target gives the carbs you need, compared against what you've planned. A progress bar and a status pill show whether each segment is on target or short, and the race header rolls it up across all segments.

Data saves in your browser. No accounts, no sync, no GPX files, no race-day mode.
